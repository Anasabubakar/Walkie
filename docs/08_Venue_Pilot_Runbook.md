# Walkie Talkie Venue Pilot Runbook

20 November 2026 · Draft 1 · Android-first, phone-hosted pilot

On-site checklist for the organizer and crew lead. The standard kit is crew phones, a powered organizer phone and reachable Wi‑Fi.

## On site

**Before doors.** Charge and plug in the organizer phone, check the router or venue SSID can reach the host, and run the preflight with WAN removed when practical. Create the event, display the QR and confirm all three channels. Walk stage, backstage, entrances and adjacent floors with real phones. Test a locked listener at distant coverage points. Brief the crew on the host status and fallback comms method. Keep a charged spare phone and an accessible QR display for a manual restart.

**During the event.** Verify the host remains active, charged and in coverage. Monitor crew count and dropped clients. If the host fails, move temporarily to the agreed fallback. Try restoring the original host first. If a spare creates a new event, have staff rescan and reselect channels. Measure time from outage to the first successful transmission.

**After the event.** End the event, expire invites and export redacted metrics if useful. Compare setup time, missed calls, host battery and temperature, worst coverage spots, and actual fallback usage. Validate whether phone hosting removes enough setup friction for event crews and when a venue-owned dedicated host makes sense. Base pricing or kit rental on observed operating costs.

## Crew briefing

Join the crew Wi‑Fi and scan the organizer QR. Pick your assigned channel. Hold to talk, wait for the chirp, say the instruction and release. If Busy appears, let the current speaker finish. Use the verified locked control only on the listed devices.

## Outage decision table

| What you see | What you do |
|---|---|
| One phone disconnected | Check its Wi‑Fi and move to coverage, then retry. Rescan if the host endpoint changed |
| Everyone disconnected | Switch to the agreed fallback. Restore the organizer phone if possible, otherwise create a new event on a spare and reshare the QR |
| Coverage gap | Move to the agreed contact point. Log the location and test the planned access-point change before relying on it |
| Locked phone misses traffic | Keep that crew handset foreground or swap to a verified handset. Record model, OS and battery mode |

Record who owns the host, who calls fallback, which channels go to each team, where the QR is displayed and how the team communicates during a restart.
